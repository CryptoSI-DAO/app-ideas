## Executive Summary

The #1 racing info app has a daily active audience of racing fans who open the app every race day for form data, results, and race cards. This research recommends extending it with a **tips marketplace** — a two-sided platform where professional and amateur tipsters can offer their picks to followers, either free or behind a paywall.

**The core insight:** monetise existing attention rather than building distribution from scratch. The racing info app is the fishing pond. The tips marketplace is where you sell the bait.

**The gap:** There is NO dedicated horse racing tips marketplace app on iOS. Tipstrr proved the two-sided marketplace model works for racing but is web-only and UK-centric. PrizePicks proved US users will engage with sports prediction apps (466K reviews) but ignores horse racing entirely.

## The Opportunity

### Why a Tips Marketplace Works on Top of Racing Info

The racing info app already solves the hardest problem in any marketplace: **distribution**. Users open the app daily, they're passionate about racing, and they're already in a 'betting mindset' when they use it. Adding a tips layer means you're monetising attention you've already captured.

### Five Reasons This Extension Makes Sense

**1. Existing Traffic Moat** — Converting free racing-info users into a tips economy is far cheaper than acquiring users from zero. The audience is already there.

**2. Natural Two-Sided Marketplace** — Tipsters get paid for their expertise. Bettors get an edge. Classic creator-economy play applied to racing.

**3. Multiple Revenue Levers** — Subscriptions, pay-per-tip unlocks, revenue share on tipster sales, and affiliate kickbacks from betting operators.

**4. Data Flywheel** — Tipsters' historical performance is public, so winners naturally rise. Leaderboards and rankings drive engagement and retention.

**5. Global Market** — Horse racing betting is $100B+ globally, with passionate fanbases in the US, UK, Australia, Japan, and Hong Kong.

## Competitive Analysis

We analysed 8 direct and adjacent competitors across web platforms, native apps, and marketplace models.

### Tipstrr (formerly Pyckio) — Market Leader

- **Model:** Subscription marketplace. Tipsters offer paid or free tips. Users subscribe to individual tipsters.
- **Key Stats:** 8.4M+ tips processed · Thousands of tipsters · Football + Horse Racing + 15+ sports
- **Strengths:** Verified performance tracking on every tip (ROI, win rate, avg monthly profit all public). Massive UK/Irish racing focus. Trusted brand since 2014.
- **Weaknesses:** Web-first with no standout native app. UX feels dated. Racing is secondary to football. US racing barely covered.
- **Revenue:** Tipster subscriptions (monthly fee, ~£5–30/tipster)

### Betaminic — Strategy Builder

- **Model:** Freemium strategy builder + tips marketplace + Telegram bot alerts.
- **Key Products:** Betamin Builder (build/verify betting strategies) · ShootingBets (odds movement alerts) · Betlamp (alerts)
- **Strengths:** Users can BUILD strategies, not just follow tipsters. Data-driven approach with 100K+ games.
- **Weaknesses:** Football-first. Racing is secondary. Web app focused. Complex for casual users.
- **Revenue:** Freemium (free strategy builder, paid pro tools at different tiers)

### PrizePicks — Prediction Market Giant (Adjacent)

- **Model:** Users make 'more/less' picks on player stats. Gamified sports predictions (DFS regulatory model).
- **Key Stats:** 4.7★ / 466K reviews on iOS · Massive US presence · $1B+ valuation
- **Relevance:** Proves US users WILL engage with sports prediction apps at scale. But ignores horse racing entirely. **Could pivot to racing** — a potential future competitor.
- **Revenue:** Take rate on entry fees

### BettingPros — Media + Picks

- **Model:** Expert picks + odds aggregation + news. Media content drives picks engagement.
- **Key Stats:** 4.8★ / 16K reviews · Affiliate bookmaker deals + premium picks subscriptions
- **Relevance:** Shows the 'content + picks' bundling works. But US sports only, no racing.

### OLBG — Community Leaderboards

- **Model:** Community forum + tipster leaderboards + tips. 4.7★ / 970 reviews.
- **Strengths:** Community-driven with forums, leaderboards, and rankings. Established brand.
- **Weaknesses:** Old-school forum vibe. App feels dated. Football-heavy. No modern UX.

### BetDAQ Tips — Exchange Cross-Sell

- **Model:** Free tips from named tipsters (Daqman, Shamrock, Multiman) tied to the Betdaq betting exchange.
- **Key Insight:** Tips are free, monetised through exchange commission. 'Tips as acquisition funnel' model. Not a true marketplace — editorial-style tips only.

## Revenue Model

The tips marketplace has five distinct revenue layers. Each can be activated independently.

| Layer | Model | User Pays For | Revenue Potential |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Subscriptions** | Monthly recurring ($2.99–$29.99/mo per tipster) | Access to a tipster's premium picks | Primary revenue stream |
| **2. Pay-Per-Tip** | À la carte unlock ($0.99–$4.99/tip) | Individual premium tips without subscribing | Secondary — impulse purchases |
| **3. Revenue Share** | Platform takes 30% of all tipster sales | Built into subscription + pay-per-tip | Scales with marketplace activity |
| **4. Affiliate CPA** | Cost-per-acquisition from bookmakers ($50–$200/signup) | Tipsters include bookmaker links; app earns on conversion | Potentially the largest revenue line |
| **5. Free/Follow** | Free tier — engagement driver | Tipsters post free tips to build reputation | Conversion funnel to paid tiers |

### User Journey

- **FREE USER** → Opens racing app → Browses race cards → Sees tips from top tipsters → Follows 3 tipsters for free
- **FOLLOWER (PAYING)** → Wants premium picks → Subscribes to tipster ($4.99/mo) OR unlocks individual tips ($1.99) → Gets push notifications
- **TIPSTER (CREATOR)** → Signs up free → Posts tips (free or paid) → Builds following → Earns 70% of all revenue
- **PLATFORM** → Takes 30% rev share + affiliate CPA + grows daily active users

## Risk Analysis

### Regulatory Risk — The Biggest One

'Pay for tips' sits in a grey area between information product and gambling advice. Mitigation:

- Clear disclaimers: *'Not investment or gambling advice. Entertainment purposes only.'*
- Age-gating (18+) on all tips content
- State-by-state awareness for US markets (online gambling legality varies)
- UK: Display GambleAware/GamCare badges (as Tipstrr does)
- Frame as **'information product'** not 'betting advice'

### Trust & Verification Risk

If tipsters can fake their track record, the marketplace collapses on day one.

- Platform verifies all results against official race outcomes
- Public ROI, win rate, and profit/loss on every tipster profile
- No self-reported results — all data comes from the platform

### Chicken-and-Egg Risk

You need quality tipsters to attract buyers, and buyers to attract tipsters.

- Recruit 3–5 known racing analysts on rev-share-only deals for launch
- Offer enhanced visibility / featured placement for early tipsters
- Allow free tips initially to build audience before gating premium content

### Competitive Risk

PrizePicks (466K reviews, $1B+ valuation) could pivot to horse racing.

**Defence:** Distribution moat. They'd need to build racing audience from scratch. You already have it. Move fast, build the two-sided network, make switching costs high through tipster loyalty.

## Recommended Approach

### Phase 1 — MVP (Weeks 1–6)

- Add 'Tips' tab to existing racing app
- Allow tipsters to create profiles and post tips (free + paid)
- Implement pay-per-tip unlock ($0.99–$4.99)
- Basic leaderboard: win rate, ROI, total tips
- Seed with 3–5 recruited tipsters on rev-share-only deals
- Add disclaimers and age-gating

### Phase 2 — Marketplace (Weeks 7–12)

- Subscription model ($2.99–$29.99/mo per tipster)
- 70/30 revenue split (tipster/platform)
- Push notifications for new tips from followed tipsters
- Verified results tracking (auto-linked to race outcomes)
- Affiliate bookmaker links on tipster pages

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 4–6)

- Expand to UK, Australia, and international racing markets
- Advanced filters: by odds range, tipster ROI, race type, track
- Social features: comments, tipster Q&A, community forums
- One-tap bet slip export to partner bookmakers
- API for tipsters to auto-post from external tools

### Estimated Build Effort

Phase 1 MVP: **4–6 weeks** (1 iOS dev + 1 backend dev)
Phase 2 Marketplace: **+4–6 weeks**
**Total: 3 months to full marketplace**
