## 6. 💰 Business Model Analysis

### Commission-Based Model
- **Rate:** 3-8% per transaction
- **Pros:** Scalable, aligned with success
- **Cons:** Requires transaction volume
- **Target:** $100K-500K/year with 500 transactions/month

### Subscription SaaS Model
- **Rate:** $10-50/month per farmer
- **Pros:** Predictable revenue, high retention
- **Cons:** Competition from free alternatives
- **Target:** 1,000 farmers = $100K-500K/year

### Advertising + Listings Model
- **Rate:** $5-20/month for premium listings
- **Pros:** Low barrier to entry
- **Cons:** Lower monetization
- **Target:** Mixed model potential

### Marketplace as a Service
- **Rate:** $500-2,000/month per cooperative client
- **Pros:** High-value B2B sales
- **Cons:** Longer sales cycles
- **Target:** 10-50 cooperatives = $50K-1M/year
