## Pitch

Sober Sips is a curated discovery app for non-alcoholic and alcohol-free drinks — wines, beers, spirits, and mocktails. With tasting notes, ratings, where-to-buy info, and a "sober curious" lifestyle guide, it's the go-to app for anyone exploring the booming NA beverage market. Not another recipe aggregator — a proper drink discovery platform.

## Target Audience
- Primary: "Sober curious" adults 25-45 exploring alcohol-free alternatives
- Secondary: People in recovery, designated drivers, health-conscious drinkers, pregnant women
- Demographics: US/UK/CA, skews female (60%), urban, iOS-first, disposable income

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Drink Catalog** — 100+ curated NA drinks (wines, beers, spirits, ready-to-drink) with photos, tasting notes, ratings, and where-to-buy links
2. **Smart Filters** — Filter by type (wine/beer/spirit/RTD), taste profile (dry/sweet/bitter/floral), occasion, price range, and dietary (vegan/gluten-free)
3. **Tasting Notes & Ratings** — Community-style ratings (1-5 stars) with structured tasting notes (nose, palate, finish) — pre-populated for MVP
4. **"Try This Instead Of"** — "Love Cabernet? Try this NA red" pairing suggestions for alcohol drinkers transitioning

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Barcode Scanner** — Scan any drink to see if it's in the database
- **Local Finder** — Find NA drinks at nearby stores/bars/restaurants
- **Sober Curious Guide** — Articles, tips, and challenges for reducing alcohol
- **Personal Collection** — Log tried drinks, favorites, wishlist
