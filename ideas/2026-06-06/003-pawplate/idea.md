## Pitch

PawPlate is a simple, friendly app that helps dog owners track and improve their pet's dental health. With cleaning reminders, a dental health score, plaque tracking logs, and vet visit notes, PawPlate makes it easy to stay on top of your dog's oral health — because bad dental health is the #1 hidden health problem in dogs, and most owners don't realize there's an issue until it's serious.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Dog owners 25-55 who are proactive about pet health
- Secondary: New dog owners, multi-dog households, pet parents who track other health metrics
- Demographics: US/UK/CA, skews female (65%), suburban/urban, iOS-first, willing to spend on pet health

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Dental Cleaning Log** — Log teeth brushing, dental chews, water additives, and dental powder with dates. Simple one-tap logging.
2. **Cleaning Reminders** — Customizable reminders (daily brushing, weekly chews, monthly powder) with push notifications
3. **Dental Health Score** — A simple 1-10 score based on cleaning frequency, product consistency, and vet checkups. Gamifies dental care.
4. **Vet Visit Notes** — Log vet dental checkups, cleanings, extractions, and notes. Photo upload for dental photos.

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Multi-Dog Support** — Track dental health for multiple pets
- **Product Recommendations** — Curated list of recommended dental products (toothpaste, chews, water additives, powders)
- **Dental Photo Timeline** — Track visual changes in your dog's teeth over time
- **Vet Report Generator** — Generate a PDF dental history to bring to vet appointments
