## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Material Guide** — Visual reference for 8 shoe materials (leather, suede, nubuck, canvas, knit/mesh, patent leather, rubber/soles, exotic skins) with identification tips
2. **Cleaning Step-by-Steps**— Illustrated cleaning procedures for each material type with time estimates and difficulty ratings
3. **Product Recommendations** — Curated list of 25+ shoe care products (cleaners, brushes, protectants, deodorizers) with what they're best for
4. **Stain Lookup** — "I got [X] on my [material]" troubleshooting: mud, oil, salt, wine, grass, ink, scuff marks
5. **Seasonal Care Tips** — Winter salt protection, summer humidity care, spring refresh, storage for off-season

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Shoe Collection Tracker** — Log your shoes with photos, purchase date, last cleaned date
- **Care Reminders** — Notifications for seasonal maintenance (re-apply waterproofing, rotate boots)
- **Sneaker Authentication Guide** — For sneakerheads: how to verify authenticity
