## Pitch
AI Shoes is a smart shoe companion app that uses computer vision and machine learning to identify, track, and recommend footwear. Users can scan their shoes to get care tips, purchase recommendations, style advice, and maintenance reminders. The app helps shoe enthusiasts, athletes, and fashion-forward individuals maximize their shoe investment through personalized insights.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Sneakerheads and shoe collectors (ages 18-35)
- Secondary: Athletes tracking performance footwear, fashion enthusiasts
- Demographics: Urban millennials and Gen Z, interested in streetwear, fitness, and tech

## Problem Statement
Shoe owners lack a centralized way to track their collection, get care instructions, and receive personalized recommendations. Existing solutions are fragmented across forums, brand apps, and manual spreadsheets. No app combines shoe identification, care guidance, and purchase recommendations in one place.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Shoe Scanner** — Use camera to identify shoe model, brand, and style; provides care instructions and value estimates
2. **Collection Tracker** — Catalog shoes with photos, purchase date, and usage stats; track wear patterns and maintenance needs
3. **Care Reminders** — Get notifications for cleaning, conditioning, and rotation based on usage frequency

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- Price tracking and resale value estimates
- Style recommendations based on collection
- Community features for sharing collections
