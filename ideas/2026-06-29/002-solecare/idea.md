# App Idea: SoleCare — Sneaker Cleaning & Shoe Care Guide

> **Pitch**: The complete sneaker maintenance reference: cleaning tutorials, material guides, product recommendations, and cleaning log — all offline.

## The Opportunity

Shoe Washing Bags (mesh bags designed for washing sneakers in washing machines) are the #2 trending product on Exploding Topics at +7,000% growth. This signals massive mainstream interest in sneaker care and maintenance. Yet a complete iTunes search for shoe care apps returns ZERO dedicated results — only sneaker shopping platforms (GOAT 2M reviews, Nike SNKRS 1.4M reviews) and laundry services.

The gap: a comprehensive sneaker care reference app that teaches owners HOW to clean and maintain their shoes.

## App Store Gap Analysis

**Search "shoe cleaning guide"** → 0 relevant results. All are shoe retailers.
**Search "sneaker care tips"** → GOAT, CONFIRMED, Nike SNKRS (all shopping).
**Search "shoe maintenance log"** → Vehicle maintenance apps (search pollution).

This is a content gap, not a shopping gap. No one has published an educational sneaker care reference for iOS.

## Content Structure (all bundled, curated)

1. **Material Guides** — Canvas, leather, suede, knit (PrimeKnit/Flyknit), mesh, patent leather
2. **Cleaning Methods** — Hand wash, machine wash (with bag), spot clean, deep clean
3. **Tool Reference** — Brushes, cleaning, protectant sprays
4. **Sole Care** — Oxidation removal, sole whitening, unyellowing methods
5. **Storage Tips** — Boot trees, silica gel, display cases, rotation schedules
6. **Cleaning Log** — User tracks when they last cleaned each pair

## Competitor Weaknesses

No direct competitors exist. Adjacent categories:
- **Laundry apps** (Poplin, FlyCleaners) — Service apps, not guides
- **Sneaker shopping apps** (GOAT, StockX) — Buy/sell only, zero care content
- **Cleaning schedule apps** (Tody, Sweepy) — Home cleaning routines, not shoe-specific

## Requirements Summary

- **Name**: SoleCare
- **Bundle ID**: com.cryptosi.solecare
- **Platform**: iOS 16+
- **Core Flow**: Browse material type → Read cleaning guide → Log cleaning session → Set reminder
- **Data**: Bundled JSON with guide content images
- **Build Time**: ~2.5 hours (mostly content curation)
- **Price**: Free with $1.99 Pro unlock (full guide library + cleaning log)
- **Category**: Lifestyle > Home & Kitchen

## Risks
- Requires visual content (photos/diagrams for each material and method)
- May need seasonal content updates (winter salt damage, summer sweat)
- Not health/beauty facing — lower impulse purchase rate (mitigated by one-time $1.99 price)
