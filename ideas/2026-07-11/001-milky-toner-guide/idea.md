## Pitch

A comprehensive Korean skincare guide focused on milky toners - the most popular toner type in K-beauty. Features product recommendations, usage routines, ingredient safety information, and a personalized toner finder based on skin type and concerns.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Korean skincare enthusiasts and beauty beginners (ages 18-35)
- Secondary: Dermatologists recommending gentle skincare, acne-prone skin sufferers
- Demographics: US/UK/Canada, iOS users, $25-50k income, interested in K-beauty

## Problem Statement

Milky toners are the #1 most popular toner type in Korean skincare, yet there's no dedicated app to help users choose the right product. Existing apps are either generic beauty guides or focus on other product types. Users struggle to:
- Identify which milky toners work for their skin type
- Understand ingredient safety (especially for sensitive skin)
- Build proper skincare routines with milky toners
- Track which products they've tried
