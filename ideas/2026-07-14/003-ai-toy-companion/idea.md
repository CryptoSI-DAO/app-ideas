## Pitch
AI Toy Companion is an interactive educational toy app that uses AI to engage children in storytelling, learning games, and creative activities. It provides personalized, adaptive content that grows with the child while maintaining strict privacy standards - bringing the benefits of AI tutoring to families without screen time concerns.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Parents of children aged 3-12 looking for educational screen time
- Secondary: Early childhood educators, grandparents, babysitters
- Demographics: Parents aged 28-45, tech-savvy, education-focused, middle-class households

## Problem Statement
Educational apps for kids are either generic flashcards or expensive physical toys. Parents want AI-powered personalization that adapts to their child's learning style, but worry about screen time, data privacy, and the quality of AI interactions with children. Existing solutions lack true personalization and parental controls.
