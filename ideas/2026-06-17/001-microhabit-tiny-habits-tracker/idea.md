## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Habit Builder** — Guided flow: pick a trigger (existing routine), choose a micro-action (30 seconds or less), set a celebration. Based on the Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP).
2. **Daily Check-in** — Simple tap to complete each micro-habit. Visual streak counter with satisfying animations.
3. **Habit Library** — Pre-built tiny habit templates organized by category: Morning, Health, Mindfulness, Work, Relationships, Evening.
4. **Progress Dashboard** — Weekly view showing completion rates, longest streaks, and total habits formed. Simple charts, no complex analytics.

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Habit Stacking** — Chain 2-3 micro-habits together into a routine
- **Reminders** — Contextual notifications based on time/location
- **Community Challenges** — Weekly tiny habit challenges with other users
- **Export Data** — CSV export for personal analysis
