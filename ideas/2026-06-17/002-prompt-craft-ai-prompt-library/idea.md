## Pitch
Prompt Craft is a curated, offline-first AI prompt library that helps users get better results from ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, and other AI tools. No more hunting through Reddit or Twitter — just browse, copy, and paste professionally organized prompts.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Professionals and creators using AI tools daily (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, etc.)
- Secondary: Students, marketers, developers, writers looking to level up their AI output
- Demographics: US/UK/Canada, 22-45, tech-savvy, iPhone users

There's no well-designed, curated, offline-capable mobile app that organizes prompts by use case, quality-rates them, and lets users save favorites. ## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Prompt Library** — 200+ curated prompts organized by category: Writing, Coding, Marketing, Business, Creative, Education, Personal
2. **One-Tap Copy** — Tap any prompt to copy to clipboard, ready to paste into any AI tool
3. **Favorites** — Save frequently used prompts for quick access
4. **Search & Filter** — Find prompts by category, keyword, or AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, etc.)

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Custom Prompts** — Add your own prompts with tags
- **Prompt of the Day** — Daily featured prompt with explanation
- **Community Ratings** — Upvote/downvote prompts by quality
- **Share Prompts** — Share via link, message, or social media

## Content & Data
