## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Product Catalog** — Curated database of low-rise sweatpants from 20+ retailers (Nike, Gymshark, American Eagle, PacSun, etc.)
2. **Fit Finder Quiz** — Quick questionnaire (body type, rise preference, fabric preference) → personalized recommendations
3. **Size Guide Overlay** — Brand-specific size charts with user-submitted fit feedback
4. **Price Comparison** — Real-time pricing across retailers with deal alerts
5. **Community Reviews** — User-submitted fit photos and reviews filtered by body type

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- AI-powered fit prediction from body measurements
- "Try on" AR overlay for visualizing rise height
- Trending styles feed with TikTok integration
- Restock alerts for sold-out styles
