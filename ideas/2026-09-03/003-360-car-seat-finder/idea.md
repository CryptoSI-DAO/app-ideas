## Pitch
Seat360 is a car seat shopping companion and safety guide — helping parents find the best 360-degree rotating car seats for their vehicles, with safety ratings, install compatibility checks, and expert reviews.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Parents/caregivers of infants and toddlers (birth-4 years)
- Secondary: Grandparents, childcare providers, expecting parents
- Demographics: 25-45, parents, vehicle owners, safety-conscious

## Problem Statement
360-degree rotating car seats are a major convenience feature but choosing the right one is overwhelming. There are dozens of models with varying safety ratings, vehicle compatibility, and price points. The "360 car seat" search trend is growing (328% growth, ET #81) but there is NO dedicated app for finding and comparing them. Existing car seat apps are either generic safety check tools (5 reviews or fewer) or retailer apps that only carry their own inventory. Parents need a single destination to compare safety ratings, check vehicle compatibility, and read expert reviews before spending $200-500 on a car seat.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Product Catalog** — Curated database of 360-degree rotating car seats from major brands (Graco, Evenflo, Britax, Chicco, Nuna, Clek, etc.)
2. **Safety Rating Dashboard** — NHTSA ease-of-use scores, IIHS ratings, crash test data per model
3. **Vehicle Compatibility Checker** — Input your car make/model/year → see which seats fit
4. **Price Comparison** — Real-time pricing across retailers (Target, Walmart, Amazon, etc.)
5. **Expert Reviews Summary** — Synthesized review data from trusted sources (NHTSA, Consumer Reports, Wirecutter)

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- Install video library per car seat model
- Recall check alerts
- Expiration date tracker
- "Is this seat right for me?" quiz (child's age/weight/height, car type, budget)

## Content & Data
- Product database: 50+ 360 car seat models, updated quarterly
- Safety data: NHTSA ratings, IIHS scores, recall history
- Compatibility matrix: Car seat × vehicle model database
- Expert review summaries: Wirecutter, Consumer Reports, NHTSA

## Monetization
- **Free tier**: Browse, basic filters, safety ratings
- **Pro ($3.99/mo or $34.99/yr)**: Full compatibility checker, price alerts, recall notifications, ad-free
- **Affiliate commission**: 3-6% on referred purchases through retailer links
- **Sponsored placements**: Car seat manufacturers pay for featured placement
