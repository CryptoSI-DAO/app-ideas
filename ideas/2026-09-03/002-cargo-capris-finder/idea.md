## Pitch
CapriFind is a shopping companion for cargo capris — helping users discover, compare, and find the best-fitting cargo capri pants across major retailers, with fit guides, style inspiration, and community reviews.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Women 18-40 interested in cargo/utility fashion trends
- Secondary: Fashion-conscious shoppers, TikTok/Instagram trend followers
- Demographics: Urban/suburban women, social media active, online shoppers

## Problem Statement
Cargo capris are experiencing a quiet resurgence (650% search growth, ET #69) but there is NO dedicated app for finding them. Major retailers (Zara, Levi's, Pull&Bear, G-STAR) carry them but buried in massive catalogs. Users have no single destination to browse, compare, and find the right fit — cargo capris sit in a niche between cargo pants and capris, and most fashion apps don't categorize them separately. Sizing varies wildly across brands, and there's no community-vetted fit data for this specific category.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Product Catalog** — Curated database of cargo capris from 15+ retailers (Zara, Levi's, Pull&Bear, G-STAR, H&M, etc.)
2. **Style Filter** — Filter by rise (low/mid/high), inseam length, pocket style, fabric, color
3. **Size Guide Overlay** — Brand-specific size charts with user-submitted fit feedback
4. **Price Comparison** — Real-time pricing across retailers with deal alerts
5. **Community Reviews** — User-submitted fit photos and style inspiration

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- AI-powered fit prediction from body measurements
- "Try on" AR overlay for visualizing fit
- Styling guide — how to wear cargo capris with different outfits
- Restock alerts for sold-out styles

## Content & Data
