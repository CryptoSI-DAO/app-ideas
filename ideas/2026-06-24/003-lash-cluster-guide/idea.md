## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)

1. **Cluster Type Guide** — Visual catalog of cluster types: length (8mm-16mm), density (light/medium/volume), curl (J/L/CC/D), material (mink/silk/synthetic), with photos and best-use recommendations

2. **Eye Shape Mapper** — Interactive guide: identify eye shape (almond, round, hooded, monolid, downturned, upturned, close-set, wide-set) → recommended cluster placement map for each zone (inner corner, center, outer corner)

3. **Application Tutorial** — Step-by-step visual guide: prep (clean lashes, apply primer), placement (tweezer technique, spacing), bonding (adhesive types, cure time), finishing (separation, curling)

4. **Look Library** — 8-10 curated lash looks: Natural, Doll Eye, Cat Eye, Wispy, Glam, Bridal, Everyday, Editorial — each with cluster map diagram and product recommendations

5. **Aftercare Guide** — How to make clusters last: sleeping positions, oil-free cleansers, brushing technique, when to remove, safe removal process

6. **Adhesive Guide** — Comparison of lash adhesive types: latex-free, sensitive, strong hold, clear vs black, with cure times and best-use cases

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)

- **AR Try-On** — Camera-based lash preview (using ARKit)
- **Removal Timer** — Track how long your clusters have been on, get removal reminders
- **Product Comparison** — Compare cluster brands by price, quality, and availability
- **Troubleshooting** — FAQ: lifting, gaps, irritation, adhesive failure
