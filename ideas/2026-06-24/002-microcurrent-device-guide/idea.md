## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)

1. **Device Database** — 15-20 microcurrent devices with full specs: current output (μA), frequency range, treatment time, probe/head type, battery life, waterproof rating, price, warranty, included accessories

2. **Skin Concern Matcher** — Interactive quiz: "What's your primary concern?" (fine lines, sagging/jowls, puffiness, acne scarring, overall toning) + "What's your budget?" → Ranked device recommendations

3. **Side-by-Side Comparison** — Compare up to 3 devices with spec-by-spec table, price/performance rating, best-for tags

4. **Clinical Evidence Guide** — Summaries of key microcurrent studies (peer-reviewed), what the science actually supports vs. marketing claims, expected timeline for results

5. **Treatment Protocols** — Step-by-step facial mapping guides for: full face lift, jawline definition, under-eye, forehead, neck. With recommended intensity and duration per device.

6. **Quick Picks** — Editor's picks: Best Overall, Best Budget, Best for Beginners, Best for Anti-Aging, Best Multi-Function (LED + microcurrent)

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)

- **Contraindications Checker** — Safety screening: pregnancy, pacemaker, metal implants, active acne, recent Botox/fillers
- **Progress Tracker** — Photo log with before/after comparison
- **Routine Builder** — Weekly treatment schedule with reminders
- **Ingredient Pairing** — Which conductive gels/serums work best with which devices
