## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)

1. **Dryer Database** — 25-30 popular hair dryers with specs: motor type (brushless/DC/AC), wattage, airflow speed (CFM), weight, cord length, technology (ionic/ceramic/tourmaline), noise level, price range, included attachments

2. **Hair Type Matcher** — Interactive quiz: "What's your hair type?" (fine, thick, curly, coily, straight, color-treated, frizzy) + "What's your priority?" (speed, volume, frizz control, budget, travel) → Ranked recommendations

3. **Comparison Tool** — Side-by-side comparison of up to 3 dryers with spec-by-spec comparison table, highlight differences

4. **Technology Guide** — Educational content: "Ionic vs Ceramic vs Tourmaline: What Actually Matters" — based on Consumer Reports findings that tech claims are often marketing

5. **Budget Filter** — Filter by price tier: Budget ($25-60), Mid ($60-150), Premium ($150-300), Luxury ($300+)

6. **Quick Picks** — Editor's picks for: Best Overall, Best Budget, Best for Curly Hair, Best for Fine Hair, Best Travel, Best Quiet

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)

- **User Reviews** — Aggregated user ratings from verified sources
- **Deal Alerts** — Track price drops (requires network)
- **Attachments Guide** — Explain diffusers, concentrators, comb nozzles, and which dryers include them
- **Heat Damage Guide** — Education on safe heat settings by hair type
