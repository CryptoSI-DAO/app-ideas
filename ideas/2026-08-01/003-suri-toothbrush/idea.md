# Suri Toothbrush

**Date**: 2026-08-01

**Score**: 7.2/10
**Trend Growth**: 7300%

## Core Problem

The Suri electric toothbrush is trending among dental professionals and health-conscious consumers, but there's no dedicated companion app to track brushing habits and care for the toothbrush itself.

## Target Audience

Dental professionals, orthodontic patients, and consumers using Suri toothbrush for hair-free oral care.

## MVP Features

- Brushing habit tracker with session logging
- Toothbrush battery and maintenance reminders
- Care schedule for cleaning and replacement parts
- Progress visualization and streak tracking
- Integration with Apple Health/Google Fit
- Educational content on hair-free brushing techniques
- Community features for tips and Q&A

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React Native
- **Backend**: Supabase
- **Monetization**: Free with optional $2.99 premium for advanced analytics
- **Distribution**: App Store & Google Play

## Success Metrics

- Daily brushing log completion > 60%
- Brush maintenance compliance > 80%
- User retention > 45% day 7
