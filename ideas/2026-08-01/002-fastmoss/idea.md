**Date**: 2026-08-01

**Score**: 7.4/10

## Core Problem

Fastmoss is trending explosively among TikTok sellers and content creators, but there's no mobile app to manage product listings, analytics, and sales across multiple platforms.

## Target Audience

TikTok Shop sellers, Amazon FBA sellers, and multi-channel e-commerce creators using fast-moving product databases.

## MVP Features

- Product catalog management with UPC/barcode scanning
- Cross-platform inventory sync (TikTok Shop, Amazon, Shopify)
- Sales analytics and performance tracking
- Supplier finder and product research tool
- Order management dashboard
- Export/import CSV functionality
- Notifications for low stock or new orders

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Flutter (cross-platform)
- **Backend**: Firebase + Cloud Functions
- **Monetization**: Freemium with premium analytics ($9.99/month)
- **Distribution**: App Store & Google Play

## Success Metrics

- Daily active users > 50% of registered sellers
- Inventory sync success rate > 95%
- Premium conversion > 5%
