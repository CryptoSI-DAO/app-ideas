# Fractional COO Onboarding Kit — Executive Operations Guide

## Core Problem
Fractional Chief Operating Officers (COOs) and operations consultants need standardized onboarding processes for their part-time executive clients. No mobile app exists to guide fractional COOs through systematic onboarding, documentation, and handoff processes.

## Solution
Mobile companion app for fractional COOs providing structured onboarding workflows, documentation templates, stakeholder mapping, and transition checklists.

## Market Validation
- **Trend**: 7,700% growth (Fractional COO)
- **App Gap Score**: 10/10 - Category pollution detected
  - Existing apps are generic HR/onboarding tools
  - No fractional executive-specific onboarding solution
  - Results show QuickBooks Workforce (191K reviews) - wrong category

## Target Users
- Fractional COOs serving startups
- Operations consultants
- Part-time C-suite executives
- Startup accelerators/VCs

## Key Features
1. **Stakeholder Mapping**
   - Visual org charts
   - Role responsibility matrices
   - Communication channels tracker

2. **Onboarding Workflows**
   - 30/60/90-day plans
   - Process documentation templates
   - KPI dashboard setup

3. **Documentation Hub**
   - SOP templates
   - Meeting agenda frameworks
   - Performance review templates

4. **Transition Handoff**
   - Exit interview guides
   - Knowledge transfer checklists
   - Succession planning tools

## Technical Approach
- **Backend**: Firebase for real-time collaboration
- **Frontend**: Flutter for cross-platform mobile app
- **Offline Support**: Sync when connection restored
- **Security**: Encrypted notes, role-based access

## Monetization
- **Freemium**: Basic templates free, advanced workflows $4.99/mo
- **Consulting Package**: $29.99/mo for agencies with white-label
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing for VC portfolios
