## Pitch

FlyMount is a buying guide app for airplane phone holders and travel mounts. With the rise in air travel and the "phone as entertainment" habit, finding a reliable mount that works with tray tables, seat backs, or your neck is frustrating. FlyMount compares the top travel phone mounts by attachment type, compatibility, stability, and price — so you never fumble with a wobbly holder at 35,000 feet.

## Target Audience

- **Primary**: Frequent travelers (business and leisure) who use phones inflight (age 25-50)
- **Secondary**: Digital nomads, flight attendants (as gift recommendations), travel gear enthusiasts
- **Demographics**: US-based, 25-50, travels 4+ times/year, middle-to-upper income

## Problem Statement

Air travel is back at record levels. Passengers rely on phones for entertainment, but holding a phone for a 6-hour flight is exhausting. Existing solutions:
- Amazon search returns 500+ results with fake reviews and copycat products
- No independent comparison tool exists
- "Best airplane phone holder" YouTube videos are sponsorship-driven
- Travelers buy cheap mounts that break or don't fit their seat type

A curated guide with categorized recommendations (tray table clamp, seat back hook, neck mount, magnetic) fills a clear niche.
