## Pitch
HatStack is a style-first hat finder that helps users discover, compare, and purchase 5-panel hats (and related styles) from independent brands, with fit guidance, style matching, and price comparison across retailers.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Fashion-conscious consumers, streetwear enthusiasts, hat collectors
- Secondary: Outdoor/activity enthusiasts seeking functional 5-panel hats
- Demographics: 18-35, urban/suburban, online shoppers, fashion app users

## Problem Statement
5-panel hats are exploding in popularity (ET #42, 6,500% growth) but there is no dedicated discovery app. Existing hat apps (Hat Club, Branded Bills) are single-brand retail apps. There's no cross-brand comparison, no fit guidance for different head shapes, and no style-based curation. The trend is driven by the return of the 5-panel hat in streetwear and the growing independent hat brand market.

- **Source 2**: TikTok/Instagram hat styling content growing rapidly; #5panelhat hashtag expanding

- **Momentum**: Rising

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Product Feed** — Curated catalog of 5-panel hats from 20-30 independent and major brands
2. **Style Filter** — Filter by color, material (canvas, corduroy, wool), pattern (solid, striped, camo), brim style
3. **Fit Guide** — Interactive tool to match head shape/size to hat style recommendations
4. **Price Comparison** — Compare same/similar hats across retailers with direct purchase links

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- Outfit pairing suggestions
- User-generated lookbook/gallery
- New arrival alerts per brand
- AR try-on (future update)
