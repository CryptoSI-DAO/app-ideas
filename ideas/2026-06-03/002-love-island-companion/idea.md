## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)

1. **Cast Directory** — Every Season 8 islander with: photo, age, hometown, bio, Instagram handle, and status (in villa/eliminated/winner). Searchable and filterable.
2. **Coupling History Timeline** — Visual timeline showing every coupling ceremony: who was with whom, when recouplings happened, who was eliminated when. The #1 feature fans want.
3. **Episode Guide** — Every episode with: air date, brief recap/spoilers flag, key moments, and which islanders were eliminated. Spoiler-tagged content.
4. **Elimination Tracker** — Current villa status: who's in, who's out, who's at risk. Visual grid of remaining islanders.
5. **Discussion Polls** — Daily polls: "Who will be eliminated next?" "Best couple?" "Most dramatic moment?" Results shown after voting closes.

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)

- Push notifications for new episode recaps
- "Spoiler-free" mode (hides elimination results until user opts in)
- Episode discussion threads (local, no backend — just prompts for user reflection)
- Season comparison (how does S8 cast compare to S7, S6?)
- Shareable "My Favorite Couple" cards for social media
- Dark mode (villa aesthetic — dark blue/pink)
