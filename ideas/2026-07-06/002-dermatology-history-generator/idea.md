## Pitch
AI-assisted tool for dermatologists to generate comprehensive patient skin history forms, track lesion progression with photos, and access standardized diagnostic criteria. Streamlines patient intake and improves diagnostic accuracy through structured data collection.

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Dermatologists, aesthetic physicians, primary care doctors
- **Secondary**: Dermatology medical students and residents
- **Demographics**: Healthcare professionals, age 25-65, clinical practice settings

## Problem Statement
Dermatology practices lack specialized tools for structured patient history collection. Manual intake forms miss critical details, and photo tracking of lesions is inefficient. Existing apps are either general medical records or consumer-focused skin tracking with no clinical terminology.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Structured History Form** — Guided intake with anatomical location mapping and lesion type selection
2. **Photo Progression Tracker** — Timeline view of lesion photos with measurement tools
3. **Diagnostic Criteria Library** — Integrated dermatological diagnostic criteria and differential diagnosis suggestions
4. **PDF Report Generator** — Export patient history with photo gallery
5. **Location Map** — Body diagram for accurate lesion location marking

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- Teledermatology integration
- Skin cancer screening checklist
- Treatment response tracking
- Patient portal sync
