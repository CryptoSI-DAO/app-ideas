## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **PDRN Science Explained** — Clear, cited explanation of what PDRN is, how it works at the cellular layer, and why salmon DNA (not human DNA) is used. Includes mechanism diagrams and research summary.
2. **PDRN Product Guide** — Curated database of PDRN products (toners, serums, ampoules, masks) with PDRN concentration, price range, where to buy, and user ratings. Organized by product type and skin concern.
3. **Treatment Protocol Library** — Step-by-step guides for different PDRN treatments: daily use (toner/serum), microneedling with PDRN, post-procedure recovery masks. Includes contraindications and safety warnings.
4. **Skin Concern Matcher** — User selects their primary skin concern (anti-aging, acne scars, hyperpigmentation, barrier repair) and gets a personalized PDRN product + routine recommendation.
5. **Before/After Gallery** — User-submitted (in v1.1) or curated clinical before/after photos showing PDRN results for different skin concerns.

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **PDRN Concentration Calculator** — Help users compare products by actual PDRN content across different formulations
- **Clinic Finder** — Map of medspas offering professional PDRN treatments (requires location, deferred)
- **Routine Builder** — Combine PDRN products with compatible ingredients (avoid mixing with what?)
- **Push Notifications** — New PDRN product alerts, research updates
