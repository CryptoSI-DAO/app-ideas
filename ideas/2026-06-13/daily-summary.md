## Top 3 App Ideas

### 🥇 #1: SubWatch — Subscription Price Watchdog — Score: 7.6/10
- **Pitch**: One-time-purchase subscription tracker that logs price changes, shows monthly/yearly spending, and reminds you before bills hit — no bank linking, no subscription required.
- **Why**: Fills a clear quality gap. All competitors either charge subscription fees themselves (ironic), require bank logins, or are abandoned. Subscription fatigue is culturally resonant and growing. Combines proven monetization ($2.99 one-time) with pure local SwiftUI implementation.

### 🥈 #2: Prompt Craft — AI Prompt Library — Score: 7.4/10
- **Pitch**: 500+ curated AI prompts across 12 categories — searchable, one-tap copy, organized, and 100% offline. "Pinterest for prompts."
- **Why**: AI tool adoption is one of the strongest trends of the decade. Prompt engineering searches are exploding. Existing iOS apps are all poorly executed (dated UI, tiny libraries, ad-supported). Content-first app that's trivially buildable.
- **Build Time**: ~2.5 hours
- **Pricing**: Free + $1.99 Pro Pack unlock

### 🥉 #3: PetLog — Pet Health Journal — Score: 7.0/10
- **Pitch**: Simple health journal for pet owners — track symptoms, medications, weight, vet visits, and vaccinations. Like a baby tracker app, but for pets.
- **Why**: Pet industry spending growing 7% YoY. "Pet as family" trend driving health-conscious ownership. Health journal niche is underserved — existing apps are either social networks or vet booking tools.
- **Build Time**: ~3 hours
- **Pricing**: Free (2 pets) + $3.99 unlock unlimited
