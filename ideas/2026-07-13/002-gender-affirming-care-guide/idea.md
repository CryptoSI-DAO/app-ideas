## Pitch
Gender Affirming Care Guide is a comprehensive healthcare resource app for transgender individuals, providing information on finding supportive healthcare providers, insurance coverage, legal rights, and step-by-step care guides. The app serves as a trusted companion for navigating the complex healthcare landscape of gender transition.

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Transgender individuals (all genders) seeking healthcare information
- **Secondary**: Partners, parents, allies supporting trans individuals
- **Demographics**: Ages 16-45, diverse locations, seeking reliable medical resources

## Problem Statement
Transgender individuals face significant barriers accessing gender-affirming healthcare. Current resources are scattered across websites, forums, and word-of-mouth. There's no centralized, mobile-friendly app that provides vetted healthcare providers, insurance guidance, legal rights information, and personalized care timelines all in one place.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Provider Directory** — Vetted healthcare providers by state/region with specialties, insurance accepted, contact info
2. **Insurance Guide** — Step-by-step insurance coverage information, claim forms, appeal letters
3. **Care Timeline** — Personalized transition timeline with resources and checklists
4. **Legal Rights** — State-by-state legal protections, documentation guides, advocacy resources
