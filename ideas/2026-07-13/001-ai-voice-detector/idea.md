## Pitch
AI Voice Detector is a mobile app that analyzes audio recordings to identify whether they were generated by AI voice synthesis or deepfake technology. Users can upload audio files or record directly in-app to receive instant analysis with a confidence score indicating the likelihood of AI generation.

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Content moderators, journalists, researchers, legal professionals
- **Secondary**: General consumers concerned about voice scams, podcasters, video creators
- **Demographics**: Ages 25-45, tech-savvy, privacy-conscious, professional or creative workers

## Problem Statement
AI voice generation technology is advancing rapidly, enabling sophisticated deepfakes that can impersonate real people. Current detection tools are either desktop-only, require technical expertise, or are not specialized for voice. There's no simple mobile app that lets anyone verify voice authenticity on-demand.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Audio Upload/Recording** — Import from files or record directly; supports MP3, WAV, M4A
2. **AI Detection Analysis** — Runs machine learning model to detect synthetic voice patterns; outputs confidence percentage
3. **Results Dashboard** — Shows detection score, confidence level, and key indicators (pitch stability, formant patterns, artifact analysis)
4. **Export Report** — Generate PDF report with timestamp, analysis results, and metadata

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Voice Comparison** — Compare against known reference audio to detect impersonation
- **Real-time Detection** — Live audio stream analysis
- **Multi-language Support** — Detect AI voices in different languages

## Content & Data
