# Cryotherapy Protocol — Cold Therapy Guide & Tracker

**Score: 7.8/10**

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive guide app for cryotherapy protocols, covering both professional cryotherapy chambers and at-home cold therapy methods. Addresses the gap between medical literature and consumer accessibility.

## Problem Statement

Cryotherapy is gaining medical acceptance but lacks consumer-friendly educational resources:
- Medical journals are inaccessible to general public
- Existing apps focus on ice baths only
- No guidance on professional cryotherapy chamber protocols
- Safety information is scattered

## Solution

A protocol-first app that educates users on:
- Whole body cryotherapy chamber protocols
- Partial body cryotherapy techniques
- Ice bath protocols (Wim Hof method)
- Cryotherapy contraindications and safety
- Recovery protocols and contraindications

## Core Features

### MVP (Phase 1 - 35 hours)
- [ ] Protocol Library: 40+ cryotherapy protocols
