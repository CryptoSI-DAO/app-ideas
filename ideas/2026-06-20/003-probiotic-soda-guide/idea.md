## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Brand Directory** — 50+ probiotic/fermented sodas with ingredients, probiotic strains, sugar content, and where to buy
2. **Comparison Tool** — Side-by-side comparison of up to 3 brands (probiotics, sugar, calories, price)
3. **Education Hub** — What are probiotics? Strains guide (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium, etc.), health benefits vs. marketing claims
4. **Find Near Me** — Search by location (manual entry, no API needed — curated store list)

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- User reviews and ratings
- Flavor profile tags (fruity, herbal, spicy, etc.)
- Dietary filters (vegan, gluten-free, low-FODMAP)
- New brand alerts
