## Differentiation

- Noise-level comparison — critical for office/apartment use (dB ratings per model)
- Desk-compatibility check — max height, footprint, weight capacity vs. common desk dimensions
- Step-count vs. speed comparison — helps users pick based on fitness goals
- Foldability rating — storage convenience scores for small spaces

## Monetization

- Affiliate links to fitness equipment retailers (Amazon, direct brands, desk retailers)
