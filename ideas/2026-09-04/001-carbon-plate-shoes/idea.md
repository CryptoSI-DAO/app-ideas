## Pitch

CarbonPlate is a shopping companion and comparison tool for carbon-plated running shoes — the exploding "super shoe" category that has reshaped competitive running. Discover, compare, and find the best carbon-plated shoes across major retailers with fit guides, ride characteristic comparisons, and community reviews. Built for runners shopping at 1,167% search growth, it's a catalog + comparison engine shipping in ~3 hours.

## Target Audience

- Primary: Runners 18-55 training for races (5K to marathon) who want the latest carbon-plated tech
- Secondary: Running enthusiasts, gearheads, fitness influencers, race planners
- Demographics: US, active lifestyle, online shoppers, running community members

## Problem Statement

Carbon-plated running shoes (Nike Alphafly, Adidas Adizero, ASICS Metaspeed, etc.) have become the dominant category in performance running — but there's no mobile tool that helps runners navigate the explosion of options. Runners must piece together reviews from YouTube, Reddit (r/running, 1.6M subs), and retailer sites to compare stack heights, plate types, drop geometries, and price points. The 5 App Store results for "carbon plate running shoes" are all generic running trackers (ASICS Runkeeper 379K, Nike Run Club 418K) or retail giants (Finish Line 280K) serving a completely different job. Zero dedicated carbon-plated shoe comparison apps exist.

## Solution

A focused shopping companion that:
- Catalogs every major carbon-plated running shoe with specs (stack height, plate type, drop, weight, price)
- Filters by use case (race day, tempo training, long run, marathon)
- Compares shoes side-by-side on key dimensions
- Aggregates community reviews from trusted running sources
- Tracks price drops across retailers
- Includes a fit-finder quiz based on foot type and gait
