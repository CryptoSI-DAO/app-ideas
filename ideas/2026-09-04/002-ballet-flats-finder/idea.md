## Pitch

FlatFind is a shopping companion for ballet flats — discover, compare, and find the best-fitting ballet flat styles across major retailers with fit guides, style inspiration, and community reviews. Built for the 957% search growth trend, it's a catalog + filters + community engine shipping in ~2 hours.

## Target Audience

- Primary: Women 18-45 looking for comfortable, stylish ballet flats for daily wear
- Secondary: Fashion enthusiasts, capsule wardrobe builders, shoe collectors
- Demographics: US, online shoppers, fashion-conscious, Instagram/TikTok active

## Problem Statement

Ballet flats have exploded back into fashion (957% search growth, ET #53) — driven by the balletcore aesthetic trend and the "quiet luxury" movement. But there's no mobile tool that helps people navigate the category: which flats are actually comfortable for all-day wear, which styles work for wide vs narrow feet, which brands are worth the price, and where to buy them at the best price. The 5 App Store results for "ballet flats" are all games (Pretty Ballerina Dancer 23K, Move Ballerina 12K) or generic retail (Level Shoes 4.8K, Aquatalia 453). Zero dedicated ballet flats shopping companions exist.

## Solution

A focused shopping companion that:
- Catalogs ballet flats across major retailers (Zappos, DSW, Nordstrom, Amazon, brand sites)
- Filters by width, material, heel height, price, and occasion
- Includes fit guides (wide foot? look for brand X; narrow foot? try brand Y)
- Aggregates community reviews and style inspiration
- Tracks price drops and sale alerts
- Features a "style quiz" to recommend flats based on personal taste
