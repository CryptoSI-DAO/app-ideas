## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Treatment Guide** — Step-by-step visual guide to Japanese head spa treatments: deep cleansing, scalp massage techniques (finger pressure points, tool-assisted), nutrient treatments, and finishing rituals
2. **Technique Library** — Illustrated scalp massage techniques with diagrams: circular pressure, kneading, tapping, and acupressure point maps for relaxation and hair growth stimulation
3. **Product Guide** — Curated recommendations for scalp care products: shampoos, serums, oils, and tools (scalp massagers, brushes) with ingredient breakdowns
4. **Science Section** — Evidence-based content on scalp health: how blood flow affects hair growth, the microbiome-scalp connection, and what treatments actually work vs. marketing hype

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Treatment Timer** — Guided treatment sessions with timer and step-by-step audio/visual prompts
- **Salon Finder** — Map of Japanese head spas in major US cities (partner with Yelp/Google Places API)
- **Progress Journal** — Track scalp health over time with photo logging and notes
- **Routine Builder** — Custom weekly/monthly scalp care routine based on hair type and concerns
