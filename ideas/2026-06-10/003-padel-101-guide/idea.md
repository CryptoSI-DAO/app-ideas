## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Official Rules Guide** — Complete, illustrated rules of padel: court dimensions, scoring system (same as tennis with modifications), serve rules, wall play rules, and common rule misunderstandings
2. **Court Diagram** — Interactive court layout showing dimensions, zones (service line, net, glass walls), and positioning guides for singles vs. doubles
3. **Beginner Drills Library** — 15-20 illustrated drills organized by skill level (beginner/intermediate/advanced): wall practice, volley drills, serve practice, lob technique, and footwork patterns
4. **Scoring Reference** — Quick-reference scoring guide with examples: how points work, tiebreak rules, match format (best of 3 sets), and DUPR rating system explanation

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Scorekeeper Mode** — Built-in score tracker for actual matches (differentiates from competitors by combining guide + scorekeeper)
- **Drill Timer** — Timed drill sessions with rest intervals
- **Court Finder** — Map of padel courts in the US (curated list, not API-dependent)
- **Video Embeds** — Links to curated YouTube technique videos (no hosting required)
- **Glossary** — Padel terminology dictionary (bandeja, víbora, chiquita, etc.)
