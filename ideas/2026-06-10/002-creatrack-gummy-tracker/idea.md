## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Daily Gummy Tracker** — One-tap logging: "I took my creatine gummies today" with customizable daily dose (e.g., 3 gummies, 5g equivalent)
2. **Smart Reminders** — Customizable daily notification: "Time for your creatine gummies 💪" with snooze and skip options
3. **Streak Counter** — Visual streak tracker showing consecutive days, longest streak, and total days tracked (gamification)
4. **Dosing Guide** — Built-in reference: recommended creatine dosing by body weight, gummy-to-powder equivalency chart, loading vs. maintenance phase guidance

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Gummy Brand Database** — Log which brand/flavor you're taking; track preferences over time
- **Progress Photos** — Optional body progress photo log correlated with supplement consistency
- **Stack Tracker** — Track other supplements alongside creatine (protein, pre-workout, vitamins)
- **Apple Health Integration** — Write workout data to HealthKit for correlation with supplement streaks
- **Widgets** — Home screen widget showing today's streak and "did you take your gummies?" prompt
