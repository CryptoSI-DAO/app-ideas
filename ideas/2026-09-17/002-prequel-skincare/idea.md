# Prequel Skincare — Brand Guide & Product Finder

## Idea Overview
**Score: 7.2/10** | **Build Time: ~2 hours** | **Pricing: Free with affiliate**

## Pitch
A dedicated skincare guide and product finder for Prequel — the rapidly growing clean skincare brand. Features a routine builder, product comparison tool, and affiliate-linked shopping guide for all Prequel skincare products.

## Market Analysis
- **Trend**: Prequel Skincare is #85 on Exploding Topics with 6,900% 5-year search growth. The brand has gone from unknown to major player in under 2 years.
- **App Gap**: iTunes search for "prequel skincare" returns 9 results — all from wrong categories (Shopping: Musely, DIME, Brandefy, DRMTLGY; Health & Fitness: Typology Paris). Zero dedicated Prequel skincare apps exist. Pollution signal confirms green field.
- **Target Audience**: Skincare enthusiasts, Prequel customers, clean beauty shoppers, routine builders
- **Competitive Landscape**: No direct competitors. General skincare apps (SkinSort, Nolla Skin) exist but don't focus on specific brands.

## Features (v1.0)
1. **Routine Builder** — Step-by-step Prequel skincare routine for different skin types (dry, oily, acne-prone, sensitive)
2. **Product Guide** — Complete catalog of Prequel products with usage instructions and reviews
3. **Product Comparison** — Compare Prequel products side-by-side on price, ingredients, and suitability
4. **Shelf Life Tracker** — Track when each product was opened and when it expires
5. **Affiliate Shop** — Direct links to purchase Prequel products with affiliate commission

## Technical Requirements
- **Platform**: iOS (React Native or Swift)
- **Backend**: Static content with affiliate links, optional user preferences stored locally
- **Dependencies**: None beyond standard SDK
- **ASO**: Title "Prequel Skincare — Routine & Product Guide", keywords: prequel skincare, prequel routine, clean skincare, skincare guide, product finder

## Monetization
- Affiliate commission on Prequel product sales (via brand affiliate programs, Amazon, Sephora, etc.)
- Optional premium ($2.99) for personalized routine recommendations and exclusive content

## Risk Assessment
- **Risk**: Brand-specific apps have limited longevity if the brand declines
- **Mitigation**: Core skincare education content (routine building, ingredient education) remains valuable regardless of brand fate
- **Risk**: Low barrier to entry
- **Mitigation**: First-mover ASO advantage for "prequel skincare" keyword
