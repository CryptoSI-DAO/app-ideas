# Cherry Bag Charm — Fashion Accessory Guide & Finder

## Idea Overview
**Score: 8.0/10** | **Build Time: ~1.5 hours** | **Pricing: Free with affiliate**

## Pitch
A guided shopping app for cherry bag charms — the trending fruit-inspired accessory that's taking over handbag styling. Features a style guide, charm pairing recommendations, and a curated product finder with affiliate links.

## Features (v1.0)
1. **Style Guide** — How to style cherry bag charms on different bag types (tote, crossbody, clutch)
2. **Charm Pairing** — Recommendations based on bag color, outfit style, and occasion
3. **Product Finder** — Curated list of cherry bag charms with affiliate links
4. **Care Tips** — Cleaning and storage guidance for delicate charms
5. **Trend Tracker** — Seasonal color and style predictions
