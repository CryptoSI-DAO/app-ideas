# Hair Glossing — Hair Shine Guide & Product Finder

## Idea Overview
**Score: 7.0/10** | **Build Time: ~2 hours** | **Pricing: Free with affiliate**

## Pitch
A guided app for hair glossing — the trending at-home hair treatment that adds shine, depth, and color refresh between salon visits. Features a treatment guide, product recommendations, and a step-by-step tutorial for achieving salon-quality gloss at home.

## Features (v1.0)
1. **Treatment Guide** — Step-by-step instructions for at-home hair glossing, including timing and application techniques
2. **Product Finder** — Curated list of hair glossing products (clear gloss, tinted gloss, semi-permanent) with affiliate links
3. **Color Matching** — Guide to choosing the right gloss shade for your hair color
4. **Before/After Tracker** — Document your hair glossing results over time
5. **Maintenance Schedule** — How often to gloss for optimal results
