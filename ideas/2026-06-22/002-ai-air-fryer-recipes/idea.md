## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Ingredient-Based Recipe Finder** — User enters available ingredients, app suggests air fryer recipes that use them
2. **Air Fryer Model Selector** — Database of 30+ popular air fryer models with wattage/basket size; recipes auto-adjust time/temp
3. **Recipe Cards** — Beautiful recipe cards with ingredients, step-by-step instructions, time, temperature, and servings
4. **Dietary Filters** — Filter by keto, vegan, gluten-free, low-carb, etc.
