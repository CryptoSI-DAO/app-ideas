## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Dosing Guide** — Science-backed creatine gummy dosing protocols (loading vs. maintenance), timing recommendations, and interaction warnings
2. **Brand Comparison Database** — Curated database of popular creatine gummy brands with serving size, creatine per gummy, price per serving, and ratings
3. **Daily Streak Tracker** — Simple tap-to-log daily creatine intake with streak counter and weekly/monthly stats
4. **Benefits & Science Section** — Educational content on creatine benefits (muscle, cognition, recovery) with cited studies
