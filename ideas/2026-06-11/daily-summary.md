- **Why:** Habit tracker market is oversaturated with complex apps. No iOS app is specifically built around the tiny habits philosophy with a free-first model. Streaks is the closest competitor but is paid-only.
- **Build Time:** ~2-2.5 hours
- **Pricing:** Free (5 habits) + $2.99 one-time unlock
