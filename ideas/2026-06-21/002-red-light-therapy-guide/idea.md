## Pitch
Red Light Therapy Guide is a clean, science-backed reference app that teaches users exactly how to use red light and LED therapy safely and effectively. Covering treatment protocols for skin health, muscle recovery, sleep, and inflammation — with wavelength guides, timing recommendations, and device comparisons. The LED face mask market is booming (+1,800% on Exploding Topics) but zero quality guide apps exist to help users actually use these devices correctly.

- Secondary: Biohackers, athletes, skincare enthusiasts, chronic pain sufferers

## Problem Statement
The LED face mask and red light therapy market is exploding, but most users have no idea how to use these devices properly. Too much exposure can be ineffective or harmful; too little does nothing. Wavelengths (630nm vs 850nm), treatment distances, session durations, and eye protection all matter — but the only information is scattered across manufacturer PDFs, Reddit threads, and YouTube videos. No dedicated mobile app consolidates evidence-based red light therapy protocols in a clean, searchable format.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Must-Have (v1.0)
1. **Protocol Library** — Evidence-based treatment protocols for: skin anti-aging, acne, muscle recovery, sleep improvement, inflammation, wound healing. Each with wavelength (nm), distance, duration, and frequency.
2. **Wavelength Guide** — Interactive chart explaining 630nm, 660nm, 810nm, 830nm, 850nm, 940nm — what each wavelength targets and why
3. **Session Timer** — Customizable countdown timer with presets for each protocol, haptic alerts
4. **Safety Guidelines** — Eye protection requirements, contraindications, pregnancy warnings, medication interactions (photosensitizing drugs)
5. **Device Comparison** — Comparison table of popular LED masks and panels (CurrentBody, Dr. Dennis Gross, Omnilux, Mito Red Light) with specs

### Nice-to-Have (v1.1+)
- **Treatment Log** — Track sessions, skin photos, progress over time
- **Dosing Calculator** — Calculate optimal dose (J/cm²) based on device power and distance
- **Research Summaries** — Plain-English summaries of key red light therapy studies
- **Routine Builder** — Create weekly treatment schedules
