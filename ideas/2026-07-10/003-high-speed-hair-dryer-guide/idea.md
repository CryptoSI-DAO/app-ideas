# High Speed Hair Dryer Guide — Buying Guide & Product Finder

## Overview
A visual guide and product finder for high-speed hair dryers—helping users discover, compare, and purchase professional-grade hair dryers with detailed reviews and tutorials.

## Core Features

### 1. Product Database
- Comprehensive catalog of 500+ high-speed hair dryers
- Advanced filtering: wattage, weight, attachments, price
- Professional salon brand comparisons (Dyson, Parlux, GHD, etc.)
- User reviews with photo verification

### 2. Buying Assistant
- Personalized recommendations based on hair type/length
- Budget calculator with financing options
- Store locator with inventory check
- Price history tracker with alerts

### 3. Tutorial Library
- Video tutorials for proper drying techniques
- Styling guides for different hair types
- Maintenance and cleaning instructions
- Professional stylist tips

### 4. Comparison Tools
- Side-by-side feature comparison
- Performance metrics (drying time, heat settings)
- Noise level ratings
- Travel-friendly filter

### 5. Community Features
- User-submitted styling photos
- Q&A forum with expert moderation
- Seasonal buying guides
- Exclusive deals from retailers

## Technical Architecture
- **Frontend**: React Native (iOS/Android)
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express
- **Database**: PostgreSQL + Elasticsearch for search
- **Media**: Cloudinary for image/video storage
- **API Integrations**: Amazon Associates, retailer APIs
- **Analytics**: Mixpanel for user behavior tracking

## Monetization Strategy
- **Free Tier**: Basic product info, limited comparisons
- **Premium ($2.99)**: Full comparison tools, video tutorials, alerts
- **Affiliate Revenue**: 4-8% commission on retailer sales
- **Sponsored Listings**: Verified brand partnerships
